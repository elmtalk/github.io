Bud&Leaf Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Bud&Leaf is an arboricultural field survey application. This privacy policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information used by the app

Bud&Leaf may use information that you enter or create while surveying, including project details, tree and vegetation survey data, map locations, photographs, drawings and exported project files.

If you choose to use location-based features, Bud&Leaf may access your device location so that you can set project locations and plot survey features on the map. If you choose to take or attach photographs, the app may access your camera or photo/file picker. These permissions are used only to provide the relevant app functions.

Where your project data is stored

The current version of Bud&Leaf does not require an account and does not upload your survey projects to a Bud&Leaf-operated cloud service. Project data and photographs are stored locally on your device and/or in the project folder that you choose. Exported files are created only when you request them.

Sharing of data

Bud&Leaf does not sell your personal information or survey data. Project files are shared only when you choose to export, copy or send them.

The app uses online mapping and web-hosting services. Requests to those services may include ordinary technical information such as your IP address, browser or device information, and the map area being requested. Those services process information under their own privacy terms. Google Play may also process information relating to installation, updates and app performance under Google's own privacy policies.

Advertising, analytics and accounts

The current version of Bud&Leaf contains no advertising, does not require user sign-in, and does not use a Bud&Leaf analytics or tracking service.

Your control of your data

You control the survey projects, photographs and exports stored on your device or in your chosen project folders. You can delete those files using Bud&Leaf or your device's file-management tools. Uninstalling the app may not remove files that you have separately saved or exported.

Children

Bud&Leaf is intended for professional and educational arboricultural use and is not directed to children under 16.

Security

Bud&Leaf is designed to keep project data under the user's control. The security of locally stored files also depends on the security of your device and chosen storage location.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Bud&Leaf adds features that change how information is handled. The current version will be published at this page.
